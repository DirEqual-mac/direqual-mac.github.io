How It Works

Step 1: Drop Folders

Drag two directories into DirEqual. The app opens them side by side as expandable trees.

Step 2: Configure Rules

Set include/exclude filters using wildcards, regex, or path matching to focus on relevant files.

Step 3: Compare

Run the scan. Differences appear instantly with colors and icons for size, date, or content changes.

Step 4: Review and Act

Expand trees, preview files, and use tabs to manage multiple comparisons effortlessly.
